FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is 963?

One app with five sections: cars, real estate, marketplace (goods), equipment and services. You can sell, rent out or offer a service, or post a request for what you need.

Is it free?

Yes. Posting listings and requests and chatting in the app are completely free.

Do I pay or get paid through the app?

No. 963 takes no payments and has no in-app payment. You agree on the deal and pay each other directly. Don’t send a deposit to someone you don’t know before you have seen the item or checked the provider.

Why isn’t my listing visible right away?

Our team reviews every listing and request before it goes live, to check the details and photos and that it is allowed. You’ll get a notification once it’s approved or if it needs changes.

I can’t find what I need. What now?

Post a request from the Requests tab: say what you need, where and when. Sellers and providers see it and can send you a quote or contact you.

What does the “Verified” badge mean?

Our team has checked the identity of the account owner or their business. It is not a guarantee of what they offer. Ratings are written by users who dealt with them.

When can I download it?

963 is launching soon on the App Store and Google Play. This page will link to both as soon as it’s live.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. You can also ask from our account deletion page.
